Who I Am

About Me

I am a Data Analyst who helps businesses make sense of their data. My work covers the full analysis process, from cleaning and validating raw datasets to analysing performance, building dashboards, and communicating findings in a way that supports decisions.

In my freelance work, I have standardised and validated financial datasets, reducing recurring reporting errors by approximately 40%. I have also automated Power BI reporting, improving reporting efficiency by about 20%, and built sales dashboards that helped teams track performance against targets and supported decisions linked to a 14% year-over-year profit improvement.

My project work spans marketing analytics, logistics operations, financial analysis, and fraud detection. This includes a statistical A/B test of more than 588,000 records, a six-page logistics performance report covering 85,410 loads and trips, and an end-to-end fraud detection pipeline using Python, Kafka, Docker, XGBoost, SHAP, and Streamlit.

I enjoy work where good analysis solves a real business problem, whether that means improving reporting, understanding customer and campaign behaviour, monitoring operational performance, or identifying patterns that deserve closer attention.

I am open to freelance, contract, and remote full-time Data Analyst opportunities.
